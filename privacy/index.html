How we collect, use and protect your information.

What information we collect

  • Contact details: your name, email address, phone number and delivery or business address.
  • Request details: what you want to buy, ship or store, product links, quantities, origin and destination, and any notes you send us.
  • Account details: your MyEGL email address and a securely stored (hashed) password. If you turn on two-step verification, your authenticator setup is stored for that purpose.
  • Order and payment records: quotations, invoices, payment status and shipment tracking. We do not see or store your full card number. Card and PayPal payments are processed by our payment provider on its own secure page.
  • Messages: emails, WhatsApp messages and calls you send to us.
  • Basic technical data: information your browser sends automatically, such as IP address and device type, which our hosting and security providers may log.

How we use it

  • To reply to your requests and prepare quotations.
  • To purchase, ship, store and deliver your goods, and to give you tracking updates.
  • To create invoices, take payment and keep financial records.
  • To run your MyEGL account and keep it secure.
  • To meet legal, customs, tax and accounting obligations.
  • To contact you about your order. We do not sell your personal data.

Who we share it with

We share only what is needed with service providers that help us run the business. These include our website hosting provider, our database and sign-in provider (Supabase), our email delivery provider (Resend), our customer records system (Zoho CRM), our payment provider (PayPal), and carriers, couriers, customs brokers, suppliers and warehouses that handle your shipment. We may also share information with authorities when the law requires it.

Transfers outside the UAE

Some providers and shipment partners are located outside the UAE. When we send your information abroad, we do so only as needed to provide the service and we take reasonable steps to protect it.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as needed to provide our services and to meet legal, tax and accounting requirements. Invoices and shipping records are normally kept for several years. When information is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it.

How we protect it

Access to your account is protected by your password and optional two-step verification. Data is encrypted in transit, and access to customer records is limited to authorised staff. No system is completely secure, so please use a strong, unique password and tell us at once if you suspect misuse of your account.

Your rights

Under applicable UAE data protection law, you may ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its use, or to withdraw consent where we rely on it. Some records we must keep by law. To make a request, email sales@ersygloballogistics.com. We will reply within a reasonable time.

Cookies and similar technologies

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies on this website. The MyEGL portal uses your browser’s session storage only to keep you signed in while you use it.

Children

Our services are for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page, with the date below.
