What is Procurement & Sourcing?

Procurement & Sourcing is for businesses that need products or materials from reliable suppliers. We handle supplier sourcing, requests for quotation (RFQs), comparison of the options and coordination of the order.

How it works

  1. Send the details

    Tell us what you need: the product, specifications, quantity and delivery destination.

  2. Get a quotation

    We source suppliers, compare the options and email you a clear quotation.

  3. Approve

    You decide which option to go ahead with. We only proceed once you agree.

  4. We take it from here

    We coordinate the order and delivery and keep you updated by email.

Common questions

What should I include in my request?

The product or material, specifications or part numbers, the quantity, the delivery destination, and your required date and budget if you have them.

Can I choose the country to source from?

Yes. State a preferred sourcing country in the form. If you have no preference, tell us and we will advise.

Is this only for businesses?

It is designed for business requirements, but you can send any sourcing request and we will tell you if we can help.

Can you also ship the goods?

Yes. ERSY GLOBAL also offers freight forwarding and warehousing, so the whole chain can be handled together.
